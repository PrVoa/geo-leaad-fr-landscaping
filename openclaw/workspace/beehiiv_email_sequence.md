## EMAIL 2: Quick Win (Day 2)

**Subject line options:**
- The 2-minute trick that saves me 3 hours/week

**Body:**

Quick one for you.

Here's something that took me way too long to figure out:

**The "3-sentence email" rule**

Every email I send follows this structure:
1. Context (why I'm writing)
2. Request (what I need)
3. Next step (specific action)

That's it. No pleasantries, no padding, no "I hope this email finds you well."

Example:

*"Hey [name], following up on our call about the Q2 campaign (context). Can you confirm the budget is approved at $5K? (request) If yes, I'll send the contract by EOD tomorrow (next step)."*

Response rate went up. Time writing emails went down.

Try it on your next 3 emails and see what happens.
