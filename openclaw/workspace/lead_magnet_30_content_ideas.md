### 🚀 HOW TO USE THESE IDEAS

**Step 1:** Pick 3-5 ideas that fit your niche
**Step 2:** Adapt the template to your specific topic
**Step 3:** Create content in your format (video, carousel, thread)
**Step 4:** Post consistently (3-5x per week minimum)
**Step 5:** Track which formats get most engagement
**Step 6:** Double down on what works

### 💡 PRO TIPS

- **Add a hook in the first 1-3 seconds** (video) or first line (text)
- **End with a CTA** — "Save this", "Follow for more", "Comment your experience"
- **Batch create** — Film/write 5-10 pieces at once
- **Repurpose across platforms** — One idea = TikTok + Reel + Short + Thread
