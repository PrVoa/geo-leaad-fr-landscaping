## QUICK START GUIDE (Included)

### Step 1: Duplicate the Template (1 min)
Click the template link → "Duplicate" → Choose your workspace

### Step 2: Set Your Weekly Focus (2 min)
Open "This Week" dashboard
Write 1 sentence: What's the theme this week?
Add your top 3 priorities for the week

### Step 3: Plan Tomorrow (2 min)
Go to tomorrow's section
Add your top 3 tasks
That's it. Don't add more.

### Step 4: Daily Use (30 sec/day)
Morning: Check your 3 priorities
Evening: Check off what you did, note what moves to tomorrow

### Step 5: Weekly Review (5 min on Sunday)
Open Weekly Review section
Answer the 4 questions
Reset for next week

### The Rules
1. **Max 3 priorities per day** — If everything is a priority, nothing is
2. **Max 5 habits** — More than 5 = guaranteed failure
3. **Weekly review is mandatory** — Skip it and the system breaks
4. **Simple > Perfect** — Resist adding complexity

## WEEKLY REVIEW QUESTIONS

1. What were my biggest wins this week?
2. What didn't go as planned? Why?
3. What did I learn?
4. What should I do differently next week?
5. What am I grateful for?
6. What's the ONE thing that would make next week great?
7. What can I delegate or eliminate?
8. How did I take care of myself?
9. Who did I help? Who helped me?
10. Rate my week 1-10. Why?
