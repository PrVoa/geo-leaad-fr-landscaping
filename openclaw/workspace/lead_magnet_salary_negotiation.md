## ⚠️ BEFORE YOU NEGOTIATE

### Know Your Numbers
- **Market rate:** Research on Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, LinkedIn Salary
- **Your minimum:** The lowest you'll accept
- **Your target:** What you actually want
- **Your ask:** 10-20% above target (leaves room to negotiate)

### Golden Rules
1. Never give a number first if you can avoid it
2. Never accept on the spot — always ask for time
3. Negotiate from a position of value, not desperation
4. Everything is negotiable (salary, bonus, equity, PTO, start date, remote days)

---

## 📞 SCRIPT 1: WHEN THEY ASK YOUR SALARY EXPECTATIONS

**Them:** "What are your salary expectations?"

**You (Deflect First):**
> "I'm flexible and more focused on finding the right fit. Could you share the budgeted range for this role?"

**If They Insist:**
> "Based on my research and experience, I'm looking at a range of $X to $Y. But I'm open to discussing the full compensation package."

**Pro Tip:** Give a range where your minimum is their starting offer.

---

## 📞 SCRIPT 2: WHEN THEY MAKE AN OFFER

**Them:** "We'd like to offer you $X for this position."

**You (Never Accept Immediately):**
> "Thank you so much! I'm really excited about this opportunity. I'd like to take some time to review the full offer. Could you send me the details in writing? When do you need a decision by?"

**Them:** Sends offer letter

---

## 📞 SCRIPT 3: COUNTER-OFFER (The Key Conversation)

**You (Via Email or Call):**
> "Thank you again for the offer. I'm very excited about joining [Company] and contributing to [specific thing you'll do].
>
> After reviewing the offer and considering [my experience in X / the market rate for this role / the scope of responsibilities], I was hoping we could discuss the base salary.
>
> Based on my research and the value I'll bring — particularly [specific skill or achievement] — I was hoping for something closer to $Y.
>
> Is there flexibility in the base salary?"

---

## 📞 SCRIPT 4: HANDLING "THIS IS OUR FINAL OFFER"

**Them:** "Unfortunately, $X is the max we can do for base salary."

**You (Negotiate Other Elements):**
> "I understand. I really want to make this work. Would it be possible to discuss [pick 1-2]:
> - A signing bonus to bridge the gap?
> - An earlier performance review at 6 months with a potential salary adjustment?
> - Additional PTO days?
> - A remote work arrangement?
> - Stock options / equity?
> - Professional development budget?"

---

## 📞 SCRIPT 5: ACCEPTING THE OFFER

**You:**
> "Thank you for working with me on this. I'm happy to accept the offer of [confirm all terms].
>
> I'm excited to join the team and looking forward to starting on [date].
>
> Could you please send the updated offer letter for my records?"

---

## 📞 SCRIPT 6: DECLINING AN OFFER

**You:**
> "Thank you so much for the offer and the time you've invested in the interview process.
>
> After careful consideration, I've decided to pursue another opportunity that aligns more closely with my career goals at this time.
>
> I really enjoyed learning about [Company] and wish you all the best. I hope our paths cross again in the future."

---

## 🚨 PHRASES TO NEVER SAY

❌ "I really need this job"
❌ "My current salary is..."
❌ "I'll take whatever you offer"
❌ "Is that the best you can do?"
❌ "I don't want to be greedy, but..."

---

## ✅ POWER PHRASES TO USE

✅ "Based on my research..."
✅ "Given my experience in..."
✅ "The value I'll bring includes..."
✅ "I'm excited about this opportunity and want to make it work"
✅ "Is there flexibility in...?"
✅ "Could we explore..."
