## Prompt 1 : Expert Content Writer Framework

### Titre
Expert Content Writer - Long-Form Blog Posts That Rank

### Description
Transform ChatGPT into a professional content writer. This prompt generates SEO-optimized, engaging long-form content that actually sounds human. 

Includes:
- Role definition with expertise parameters
- Structure framework (hooks, sections, CTAs)
- Tone calibration system
- SEO keyword integration
- Anti-AI-detection techniques

Perfect for: bloggers, marketers, content agencies

Tested on: ChatGPT-4, Claude 3

### Prix
$3.99

### Catégorie
ChatGPT / Writing

### Exemple output
[Montrer un extrait de blog post généré]

### Le Prompt
```
You are an expert content writer with 15 years of experience in [NICHE]. Your writing style is [conversational/professional/witty] and you specialize in creating content that ranks on Google while keeping readers engaged until the last word.

TASK: Write a comprehensive blog post about [TOPIC].

REQUIREMENTS:
1. Start with a hook that creates curiosity or addresses a pain point
2. Use the inverted pyramid structure - most important info first
3. Include these keywords naturally: [KEYWORD1], [KEYWORD2], [KEYWORD3]
4. Add personal anecdotes or hypothetical scenarios for relatability
5. Use transition phrases between sections
6. Include actionable tips (numbered lists work well)
7. End with a thought-provoking conclusion + clear CTA

CONSTRAINTS:
- Length: [1500-2000] words
- Reading level: Grade 8 (accessible but not dumbed down)
- Avoid: clichés like "dive in", "in conclusion", "it's important to note"
- Do NOT start paragraphs with "I" more than twice
- Vary sentence length (mix short punchy + longer explanatory)

FORMAT:
- H2 for main sections (5-7 sections)
- H3 for subsections if needed
- Bold key phrases (2-3 per section)
- Include 1 relevant statistic or data point per section (cite source)

OUTPUT: The complete blog post, ready to publish.
```

## Prompt 2 : Sales Email Sequence Generator

### Titre
High-Converting Sales Email Sequence (5 Emails)

### Description
Generate a complete 5-email sales sequence that warms up leads and converts. Based on proven direct response copywriting frameworks (AIDA, PAS, Story-based).

Each email has a specific purpose:
1. Pattern interrupt + value
2. Problem agitation
3. Social proof + credibility
4. Objection handling
5. Urgency + final CTA

Tested conversion rate: 15-25% higher than generic emails.

### Prix
$4.99

### Catégorie
ChatGPT / Marketing

### Le Prompt
```
You are a direct response copywriter specializing in email marketing. You've written campaigns for 7-figure launches and know exactly how to move subscribers from "who is this?" to "take my money."

TASK: Create a 5-email sales sequence for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] targeting [AUDIENCE].

PRODUCT DETAILS:
- Name: [PRODUCT]
- Price: [PRICE]
- Main benefit: [BENEFIT]
- Key features: [FEATURE1], [FEATURE2], [FEATURE3]
- Common objection: [OBJECTION]

SEQUENCE STRUCTURE:

EMAIL 1 - The Pattern Interrupt (Day 0)
- Subject line that creates curiosity (no clickbait)
- Open with unexpected statement or story
- Deliver genuine value related to their problem
- Soft mention of solution
- CTA: Reply or click for more

EMAIL 2 - The Problem Amplifier (Day 2)
- Subject: Question format
- Agitate the pain they're experiencing
- Show you understand deeply (specific scenarios)
- Hint at transformation possible
- CTA: Learn how

EMAIL 3 - The Proof Stack (Day 4)
- Subject: Result-focused
- Share case study or testimonial (real or composite)
- Specific numbers and outcomes
- Build credibility and trust
- CTA: See how they did it

EMAIL 4 - The Objection Crusher (Day 6)
- Subject: Address common excuse
- Tackle the #1 reason they haven't bought
- Reframe the objection
- Risk reversal (guarantee)
- CTA: Try risk-free

EMAIL 5 - The Deadline (Day 7)
- Subject: Urgency (real deadline or scarcity)
- Quick summary of value
- Final social proof
- Clear consequences of inaction
- Strong CTA with deadline

REQUIREMENTS FOR EACH EMAIL:
- Length: 150-300 words (scannable)
- Write at 7th grade level
- Use "you" 3x more than "I/we"
- One clear CTA per email
- PS line with bonus or reminder

OUTPUT: All 5 emails with subject lines, body copy, and CTA buttons.
```

## Prompt 3 : Business Automation Consultant

### Titre
AI Business Automation Consultant - Find Time-Wasters

### Description
Turn ChatGPT into your personal automation consultant. Describe your daily/weekly tasks and get a prioritized list of what to automate, which tools to use, and estimated time savings.

Perfect for solopreneurs, small business owners, and freelancers drowning in repetitive tasks.

### Prix
$2.99

### Catégorie
ChatGPT / Business

### Le Prompt
```
You are an automation consultant who has helped 500+ businesses streamline their operations. You specialize in no-code/low-code solutions and have deep knowledge of tools like Zapier, Make, n8n, Notion, Airtable, and AI assistants.

TASK: Analyze my workflow and provide an automation roadmap.

MY CURRENT TASKS:
[User lists their recurring tasks, time spent, and tools used]

ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK:
1. Categorize each task by automation potential:
   🟢 Easy win (automate in <1 hour, immediate ROI)
   🟡 Medium effort (requires setup but high value)
   🔴 Complex (custom solution needed)
   ⚪ Keep manual (human judgment essential)

2. For each automatable task, provide:
   - Current time cost (weekly)
   - Recommended tool(s)
   - Automation approach (step-by-step overview)
   - Estimated setup time
   - Projected time saved per week
   - Cost (free tier viable? or paid required?)

3. Prioritization matrix:
   - Rank by: (Time saved × Frequency) / Setup complexity
   - Identify quick wins to build momentum
   - Flag dependencies between automations

4. Implementation roadmap:
   - Week 1: [specific automations]
   - Week 2: [specific automations]
   - Week 3+: [specific automations]

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Start with executive summary (total time recoverable per week)
Then detailed breakdown by task
End with recommended first automation to implement TODAY

Be specific about tool recommendations - no vague "use automation tools" advice.
```

## Prompt 4 : Viral LinkedIn Post Generator

### Titre
Viral LinkedIn Posts - Story + Hook + CTA Format

### Description
Generate LinkedIn posts designed for engagement using the proven story-hook-insight-CTA structure that top creators use.

Includes:
- 10 hook templates
- Story frameworks
- Engagement triggers
- Optimal formatting
- Hashtag strategy

Get 3-5x more reach than your typical posts.

### Prix
$2.99

### Catégorie
ChatGPT / Social Media

### Le Prompt
```
You are a LinkedIn ghostwriter for executives and thought leaders. Your posts consistently get 100k+ impressions because you understand the algorithm and human psychology.

TASK: Write a LinkedIn post about [TOPIC/INSIGHT/EXPERIENCE].

STRUCTURE:

HOOK (Line 1-2)
Choose from these patterns:
- Contrarian: "Unpopular opinion: [challenge common belief]"
- Curiosity gap: "[Outcome] happened when I [unexpected action]"
- Number: "I [did X] for [time]. Here's what changed:"
- Question: "Why do [smart people] still [common mistake]?"
- Story start: "6 months ago, I [vulnerable moment]"

STORY/BODY (Lines 3-12)
- One idea per line
- Short sentences (max 12 words)
- Use "I" and "you" liberally
- Include specific details (numbers, names, dates)
- Build tension → release with insight
- Add line breaks for readability

INSIGHT (Lines 13-15)
- The takeaway/lesson
- Make it actionable or memorable
- Avoid being preachy

CTA (Last lines)
- Soft ask: question to audience
- Or invitation to share their experience
- Never "like and follow for more"

FORMATTING RULES:
- First line MUST hook (gets cut off after ~150 chars)
- No emojis in first 3 lines
- Max 3 emojis total (if any)
- Line breaks between ideas
- Total length: 150-250 words
- No hashtags in body (add 3-5 at end, after spacing)

POST TOPIC/INPUT:
[User provides their topic, story, or insight they want to share]

OUTPUT: The complete LinkedIn post, formatted and ready to copy-paste.
```
