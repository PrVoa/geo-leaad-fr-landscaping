## 🔧 THE PROMPT

```
You are an expert cold email copywriter who has written campaigns with 40%+ reply rates. You understand that most cold emails fail because they're too long, too focused on the sender, and have weak CTAs.

Create a cold email outreach sequence for the following:

MY SERVICE/PRODUCT: [SERVICE]
TARGET RECIPIENT: [RECIPIENT - e.g., "ecommerce founders doing $1M+/year"]
MY UNIQUE VALUE: [VALUE PROP - what makes you different]

Generate:

## EMAIL 1 - Initial Outreach
- 5 subject line options (pattern-interrupt style, no spam triggers)
- Email body (under 80 words)
- Structure: Hook → Relevance → Value → Soft CTA

## EMAIL 2 - Follow-up (3 days later)
- Subject: Re: original
- Body: Short, adds new value, asks simple question

## EMAIL 3 - Breakup Email (7 days later)
- Subject: Pattern interrupt
- Body: Ultra short, creates urgency without being pushy

For each email:
- Use conversational tone
- No fluff or corporate speak
- Focus 80% on them, 20% on you
- CTA = low commitment (quick question, not "book a call")

Also provide:
- 3 personalization hooks I can research
- A/B test suggestion for Email 1
```

## 📸 EXAMPLE OUTPUT

**Service:** AI automation consulting for ecommerce
**Target:** Ecommerce founders with 10+ employees
**Value:** Save 20 hours/week on repetitive tasks

**EMAIL 1:**

**Subject Lines:**
1. quick question about [COMPANY]
2. saw your [RECENT THING] - idea
3. stealing 20 hours back
4. [FIRST NAME] - wrong person?
5. your team's copy-paste problem

**Body:**
```
Hi [NAME],

Noticed [PERSONALIZATION HOOK].

Quick question: how much time does your team spend on repetitive tasks like order processing, customer emails, or inventory updates?

Most ecommerce teams I work with waste 20+ hours/week on stuff AI can handle.

Worth a 5-min chat to see if it applies to you?

[YOUR NAME]
```

**EMAIL 2 (Day 3):**
```
Subject: Re: quick question about [COMPANY]

[NAME],

Not trying to spam you - just wanted to share a quick win.

A similar brand ([COMPETITOR]) automated their returns processing last month. Saved them 8 hours/week.

Is that something you'd want for [COMPANY]?

[YOUR NAME]
```

**EMAIL 3 (Day 10):**
```
Subject: closing the loop

[NAME],

I'll assume this isn't a priority right now - totally get it.

If repetitive tasks ever become a pain point, I'm an email away.

Cheers,
[YOUR NAME]

PS - No hard feelings, I'll stop bugging you :)
```
