## 🔧 THE PROMPT

```
You are a world-class conversion copywriter who has written landing pages for 7-figure launches. You understand that great landing page copy follows a structure: grab attention, identify the pain, present the solution, prove it works, handle objections, and make the action irresistible.

Create complete landing page copy for:

PRODUCT/SERVICE: [PRODUCT NAME AND DESCRIPTION]
TARGET AUDIENCE: [WHO IS THIS FOR]
KEY FEATURES: [LIST 3-5 FEATURES]
DESIRED ACTION: [CTA - e.g., "Sign up for free trial" or "Book a call"]
PRICE POINT (if applicable): [PRICE]

Generate the following sections:

## ABOVE THE FOLD
- Headline (benefit-focused, specific, under 10 words)
- Subheadline (expands on headline, adds curiosity or proof)
- Primary CTA button text
- Supporting micro-copy under button (risk reducer)
- 3 alternative headlines for A/B testing

## PROBLEM SECTION
- Header that calls out the pain
- 3-4 bullet points describing the pain vividly
- Agitation sentence that makes status quo unbearable

## SOLUTION SECTION
- Transition sentence
- How the product solves each pain point
- "What if..." future pacing paragraph

## FEATURES → BENEFITS
For each feature provided, write:
- Feature statement
- "Which means..." benefit
- Emotional outcome

## SOCIAL PROOF SECTION
- Section header
- 3 testimonial prompts (what they should say)
- Metrics/numbers to highlight

## FAQ SECTION
Write 5 FAQs that handle these objection types:
1. Price objection
2. "Will this work for me?" objection
3. Time/effort objection
4. Trust/credibility objection
5. "What if it doesn't work?" objection

## FINAL CTA SECTION
- Recap headline
- Urgency or scarcity element (if authentic)
- Final CTA
- Risk reversal statement

Make copy conversational, specific, and focused on transformation over features.
```
