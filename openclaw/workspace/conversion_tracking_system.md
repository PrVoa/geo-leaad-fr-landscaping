# 📊 PLATA CONVERSION TRACKING SYSTEM

## Purpose
Track all revenue, conversions, and marketing metrics without paid tools.

---

## 🔗 TRACKING LINKS STRATEGY

### UTM Parameters for Each Channel

**Base URL Structure:**
`?utm_source=[platform]&utm_medium=[type]&utm_campaign=[product]`

### Platform-Specific Links:

**PromptBase Prompts:**
- No UTM needed (sales tracked in PromptBase dashboard)
- Track: Units sold, revenue, which prompts perform

**Gumroad Products:**
- Built-in analytics shows traffic sources
- Use product-specific links in different channels:
  - Reddit: `gumroad.com/l/[product]?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=[subreddit]`
  - LinkedIn: `gumroad.com/l/[product]?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=post`
  - Beehiiv: `gumroad.com/l/[product]?utm_source=newsletter`
  - Carrd: `gumroad.com/l/[product]?utm_source=carrd`

**Ko-fi Products:**
- Same UTM approach
- Track commission page views vs sales

## 🚨 ALERT THRESHOLDS

### Positive Signals (Double Down)
- Product gets 3+ sales in 24h → Create similar product
- Channel drives 5+ sales → Increase posting frequency
- Free→Paid conversion >10% → Expand funnel

### Warning Signs (Investigate)
- 100+ views, 0 sales → Price or copy problem
- <1% email open rate → Subject line problem
- No traffic from channel after 5 posts → Wrong audience

### Pivot Triggers
- 0 sales after 50+ product views → Major revision needed
- Channel dead after 2 weeks → Move to next channel
- Product type fails 3x → Abandon category
