LinkedIn Viral Post Generator - Hook Formulas + Engagement Triggers


## LISTING 4: Customer Research Interview Questions
**Prix**: $2.49
**Catégorie**: ChatGPT > Business
**Modèle**: GPT-4

### Titre
Customer Research Interview Script - Uncover Real Pain Points & Buying Triggers

### Description
Generate professional customer research interview scripts. Based on "The Mom Test" methodology and Jobs-to-be-Done framework.

**Perfect for:**
- Product managers validating features
- Founders doing customer discovery
- Marketing teams understanding messaging
- UX researchers conducting user interviews

**Includes:**
- Warm-up questions
- Pain point discovery
- Current solution mapping
- Buying trigger questions
- Willingness to pay exploration
- Follow-up probes

### Prompt (ACTUAL)
```
You are a customer research expert trained in "The Mom Test" methodology and Jobs-to-be-Done framework.

Create a customer research interview script for:
- MY PRODUCT/IDEA: [description]
- TARGET CUSTOMER: [who they are]
- RESEARCH GOAL: [what I want to learn]
- INTERVIEW LENGTH: [15/30/45 minutes]

Generate a complete interview script following these principles:

**RULES (from The Mom Test):**
- Never ask "would you buy this?" (people lie)
- Ask about past behavior, not future intentions
- Ask about specific instances, not generalities
- Let them talk 90%, you talk 10%
- Follow the energy (dig deeper on emotional topics)

**STRUCTURE:**

1. WARM-UP (2-3 questions)
- Build rapport
- Establish context of their role/situation

2. PROBLEM DISCOVERY (5-7 questions)
- "Tell me about the last time you [relevant activity]..."
- "What's the hardest part about [problem area]?"
- "Walk me through how you currently handle [process]..."
- Probe: "Why is that?" / "Can you give me an example?"

3. CURRENT SOLUTION MAPPING (3-4 questions)
- What they've tried
- What they're paying (time/money)
- What's missing

4. PRIORITY & URGENCY (3-4 questions)
- How important is solving this?
- What would happen if nothing changed?
- What have they already tried?

5. BUYING CONTEXT (2-3 questions)
- Who else is involved in decisions?
- What would success look like?
- Budget/timeline hints (indirect)

6. CLOSE
- Ask for referrals to others with similar challenges
- Permission to follow up

Include specific probe questions for when you need to dig deeper.
Format: Ready-to-print script with timing guides.
```
