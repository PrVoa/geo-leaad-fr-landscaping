## Deep Dive: Each Tool and Why

### 1. Notion — The Central Hub ($10/month)

Everything lives in Notion. Client info, project tracking, meeting notes, content calendar, templates.

**What I love:**
- One tool instead of five
- Templates mean I don't start from scratch
- Offline mode (finally)
- Database relationships are powerful

**What I don't love:**
- Mobile app is slow
- Learning curve is real

**My setup:**
- Client database (linked to projects)
- Project tracker (kanban + timeline views)
- Content calendar
- Meeting notes template
- Resource library

**Pro tip:** Don't build from scratch. Find a template that's 70% right and customize it. I sell mine, but there are good free ones too.

### 2. Beehiiv — Email Marketing ($0)

Switched from ConvertKit to Beehiiv last year and haven't looked back.

**Why Beehiiv over others:**
- Free tier is actually usable (2,500 subscribers)
- Clean design without coding
- Built-in referral program
- Analytics that make sense

**What I use it for:**
- Weekly newsletter
- Lead magnet delivery
- Automated welcome sequence
- Subscriber segmentation

**What's missing:**
- Advanced automations need paid tier
- Fewer integrations than ConvertKit

If you're just starting, Beehiiv free is more than enough. I only upgraded when I hit 2,000 subscribers.

### 3. Canva Pro — Design Without Designers ($13/month)

I'm not a designer. Canva Pro makes me look like one.

**What I create:**

- Lead magnet PDFs
- Presentation decks
- Simple logos and brand assets

**Why Pro over Free:**

- Brand kit keeps things consistent
- Resize for multiple platforms
- Premium templates save hours

**What I'd use instead if broke:**

### 4. Carrd — Landing Pages ($19/year)

Yes, per year. Not month.

**Why Carrd:**
- Stupid simple
- Loads fast
- Looks professional
- Connects to everything

**What I build with it:**
- Lead magnet landing pages
- Simple service pages
- Link-in-bio pages
- Coming soon pages

**Limitations:**

- Limited functionality for complex sites
- No blog capability

For simple landing pages that just work, nothing beats Carrd's price/quality ratio.

### 5. Make (Integromat) — Automation ($0 free tier)

Switched from Zapier to Make for one reason: the free tier is more generous.

**What I automate:**
- New form submission → Notion database
- New email subscriber → Welcome email + tag
- Invoice sent → Slack notification
- New client → Add to Google Sheet CRM

**Why Make over Zapier:**
- More operations on free tier
- Visual workflow builder is better
- More powerful data manipulation

**When to upgrade:**
When you're running 1,000+ operations/month. Until then, free works.

---

### 6. Google Sheets — The Underrated CRM ($0)

Don't buy a CRM until you have 50+ active leads.

**My simple setup:**
- Name, email, company
- Lead source
- Status (prospect/contacted/proposal/client/lost)
- Value (if known)
- Last contact date
- Next follow-up

**Why this works:**
- You already know how to use it
- Free forever
- Easy to export when you need a real CRM
- Filters and conditional formatting = good enough

**When to upgrade:**
When manual entry takes >1 hour/week or you're missing follow-ups regularly.

### 7. Wave — Invoicing ($0)

Free invoicing that doesn't look free.

**What I use it for:**
- Professional invoices
- Payment tracking
- Basic financial reports
- Receipts scanning

**Limitations:**
- Payment processing fees if accepting cards
- Limited customization

For simple invoicing, Wave is more than enough. I've sent $50K+ through it without paying a cent in software fees.

### 8. ChatGPT Plus — AI Assistant ($20/month)

The biggest productivity boost in my stack.

**How I use it:**
- First drafts of everything
- Email responses
- Content repurposing
- Client research
- Problem solving

**Why Plus over Free:**
- GPT-4 is significantly better
- No usage limits during busy periods
- Faster response times
- Plugins and advanced features

**ROI calculation:**
If ChatGPT saves me 5 hours/week and my time is worth $50/hour, that's $250/week value for $20/month. No-brainer.

## What I Deliberately Don't Pay For

**Project management beyond Notion:** Don't need Asana, Monday, ClickUp if Notion works.

**Fancy CRM:** HubSpot, Pipedrive, etc. are overkill under 50 leads.

**Scheduling tools:** Calendly free or Cal.com free. Don't pay $12/month to save 3 minutes.

**Grammar tools:** ChatGPT handles this. Grammarly Premium is unnecessary.

**Stock photos:** Unsplash and Pexels are free. Canva Pro includes some too.

## The Upgrade Path

**At $0-1K/month revenue:**
Use all free tiers. Focus on getting clients, not optimizing tools.

**At $1-3K/month revenue:**
- Upgrade Notion (worth it for the extra features)
- Add ChatGPT Plus (if you haven't already)
- Maybe Canva Pro

**At $3K+/month revenue:**
- Consider Beehiiv paid for automations
- Maybe upgrade Make/Zapier
- Consider actual CRM if leads are overwhelming
