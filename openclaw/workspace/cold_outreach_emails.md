# Cold Outreach Email Templates — Plata Automation

## Target Audiences

### Audience 1: Marketing Agencies
**Pain points:** Content creation bottleneck, repetitive tasks, client reporting
**Solution angle:** Automation + AI prompts for content

### Audience 2: Solopreneurs/Coaches
**Pain points:** Wearing too many hats, no time for delivery
**Solution angle:** Workflow automation, client onboarding

### Audience 3: E-commerce Store Owners
**Pain points:** Product descriptions, customer service, inventory
**Solution angle:** AI copywriting, automation

I help [THEIR ROLE] automate [SPECIFIC TASK] so they can focus on [WHAT THEY ACTUALLY WANT TO DO].

Would it be worth a 15-minute call to see if I can do something similar for you?

No pitch, just a quick audit of what's automatable in your workflow.

Best,
Plata

P.S. Even if we don't work together, I'll send you one free automation idea you can implement immediately.

- Calls booked
- Conversion to paid

### Target Numbers (Week 1):
- 20 personalized emails
- 10 LinkedIn connections

- Goal: 1-2 calls

- Gmail (sending)

## Finding Prospects

### Where to Look:
1. **Twitter** - People posting about being overwhelmed, too many tasks
2. **LinkedIn** - Marketing managers, agency owners, coaches
3. **Product Hunt** - Recent launchers (they need automation)
4. **IndieHackers** - Solopreneurs building products
5. **Shopify App Store reviews** - Store owners mentioning manual work

### Ideal Prospect Signals:
- Posts about "working 60 hours"
- Complaints about repetitive tasks
- Multiple social platforms active
- Growing team/business
- Recently funded (if startup)

### "Send me more info"
"Sure! Here's a quick overview: [CARRD LINK]. And here's a free guide showing my approach: [LEAD MAGNET]. If any of that resonates, let me know and we can jump on a quick call to discuss your specific situation."
