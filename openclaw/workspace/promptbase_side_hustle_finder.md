# PromptBase Listing: Side Hustle Finder

## Basic Info
**Title:** Find Your Perfect Side Hustle — Personalized AI Analysis
**Price:** $2.99
**Category:** Business
**Model:** GPT-4 / GPT-3.5

---

## Description (for PromptBase)

Stop wasting time scrolling through generic "100 side hustle ideas" lists.

This prompt analyzes YOUR specific situation and recommends the 3 BEST side hustles based on:

✅ Your existing skills (even hidden ones)
✅ Your realistic available time
✅ Your income goals and timeline
✅ Your risk tolerance and budget
✅ Your interests and preferences

**What you get:**

- Top 3 personalized recommendations ranked by fit
- Why each matches YOUR profile
- Realistic income timeline (no BS hype)
- Step-by-step getting started guide
- Common mistakes to avoid
- One action to do TODAY

**Made for:**
- People with full-time jobs wanting extra income
- Parents with limited time windows
- Anyone overwhelmed by options
- People who've tried and failed before

No "become a day trader" or "start dropshipping" generic advice. Real recommendations based on YOUR situation.

---

## The Prompt

```
You are a side hustle consultant who has helped 1000+ people find sustainable additional income streams. You're known for being realistic, not hyping unrealistic expectations.

Analyze my profile and recommend the 3 BEST side hustles for me:

=== MY PROFILE ===

SKILLS I HAVE:
- [List 3-5 skills - can be professional (Excel, writing, design) or personal (organizing, talking to people, fixing things)]

TIME AVAILABLE:
- Hours per week: [X hours]
- When: [evenings after 7pm / weekends only / early mornings / flexible]
- Consistency: [same every week / varies wildly]

INCOME GOALS:
- Target: $[X] per month extra
- Timeline: [need money in 30 days / can build over 3-6 months / playing long game]
- Purpose: [pay bills / save for something specific / build wealth / just extra spending money]

CONSTRAINTS:
- Budget to invest upfront: $[0-50 / 50-200 / 200-500 / 500+]
- Comfort being visible online: [very comfortable / somewhat / prefer anonymous]
- Technical skills: [struggle with tech / can figure things out / very tech savvy]
- Have a car/can travel locally: [yes / no]
- Physical limitations: [none / prefer not physical / need to sit]

WHAT I ENJOY (helps match personality):
- [List 2-3 things you genuinely enjoy doing]

WHAT I HATE:
- [List 1-2 things you definitely don't want to do]

=== YOUR ANALYSIS ===

Based on this profile, provide:

## 🏆 TOP 3 SIDE HUSTLE RECOMMENDATIONS

For each (ranked #1 being best fit):

### [Side Hustle Name]

**Why it fits you:**
[Specific reasons based on my profile]

**Income potential at your time commitment:**
- Month 1-3: $X-Y
- Month 6: $X-Y  
- Month 12+: $X-Y

**Time to first dollar:** [X days/weeks]

**Startup cost:** $[X]

**Difficulty:** [⭐ to ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐]

**Scaling potential:** [Low / Medium / High]

---

## 🚀 HOW TO START (for your #1 recommendation)

**This week:**
- Day 1: [Specific action]
- Day 2-3: [Next step]
- Day 4-7: [Goal to hit]

**Week 2-4 milestones:**
- [Milestone 1]
- [Milestone 2]
- [Milestone 3]

**End of Month 1 target:** [Specific measurable goal]

---

## ⚠️ RED FLAGS TO AVOID

3 common mistakes beginners make with your #1 recommendation:
1. [Mistake + how to avoid]
2. [Mistake + how to avoid]
3. [Mistake + how to avoid]

---

## ✅ YOUR ONE ACTION FOR TODAY

Do this in the next 30 minutes:
[Specific, concrete action that takes less than 30 minutes]

---

Remember: Be realistic. No hype. If someone has 3 hours/week and wants $5K/month, tell them it's unrealistic and suggest a better goal.
```

## Tips for Buyers

- Be brutally honest about your available time (overestimating is the #1 reason side hustles fail)
- Include "hidden" skills like being organized, good with people, or patient
- If you hate something, SAY IT — bad matches lead to quitting
- Run the prompt again in 3 months as your situation evolves
