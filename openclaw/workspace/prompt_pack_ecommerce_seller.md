## PROMPT 1: Product Listing Optimizer

**Titre:** E-commerce Product Listing Optimizer — Amazon/Etsy/Shopify

**Description:**
Transform any product into a conversion-optimized listing. Works for Amazon, Etsy, Shopify, eBay. Generates SEO-optimized title, bullet points, description, and keywords.

**Prompt:**
```
You are an elite e-commerce copywriter who has written 5,000+ product listings that generated $50M+ in sales. You understand SEO, conversion psychology, and platform-specific algorithms.

I need you to create an optimized product listing.

**Product Details:**
- Product name/type: [YOUR PRODUCT]
- Key features: [LIST 3-5 FEATURES]
- Target customer: [WHO BUYS THIS]
- Price point: [BUDGET/MID/PREMIUM]
- Platform: [AMAZON / ETSY / SHOPIFY / EBAY / OTHER]
- Competitors to outperform: [Optional: paste competitor listing URL or description]

Generate a complete listing:

1. **TITLE** (platform-optimized)
   - Primary keyword front-loaded
   - Secondary keywords included naturally
   - Character count noted
   
2. **BULLET POINTS / KEY FEATURES** (5 points)
   - Benefit-first format
   - Includes emotional trigger
   - Technical specs where relevant
   - Social proof language
   
3. **DESCRIPTION** (150-300 words)
   - Hook opening
   - Problem-agitation-solution structure
   - Sensory language
   - Trust elements
   - Clear CTA
   
4. **SEO KEYWORDS** (20 keywords)
   - Primary (3)
   - Secondary (7)  
   - Long-tail (10)
   
5. **A+ CONTENT SUGGESTIONS** (for Amazon)
   - Module recommendations
   - Image text suggestions
   
6. **COMPETITOR GAPS** 
   - What your listing does that competitors don't mention

Make it scannable, benefit-focused, and conversion-optimized. No fluff.
```

## PROMPT 2: Product Review Response Generator

**Titre:** E-commerce Review Response System — Turn Reviews Into Sales

**Description:**
Professional responses to customer reviews (positive AND negative) that build trust and convert future buyers. Includes damage control templates.

**Prompt:**
```
You are a customer experience specialist who has managed reviews for Fortune 500 e-commerce brands. You turn negative reviews into loyal customers and positive reviews into marketing assets.

**Review to respond to:**
[PASTE REVIEW HERE]

**Context:**
- Star rating: [1-5]
- Product: [What they bought]
- Issue mentioned (if any): [Summarize]
- Is the complaint valid?: [YES/NO/PARTIALLY]
- Any compensation offered?: [YES - describe / NO]

Generate a response that:

FOR NEGATIVE REVIEWS (1-3 stars):
1. Acknowledge the specific issue (no generic "we're sorry")
2. Take ownership without excessive apology
3. Explain what happened (if relevant) without making excuses
4. Offer specific resolution
5. Invite offline conversation for complex issues
6. End with forward-looking statement
7. Keep under 150 words
8. Include subtle trust rebuild for future readers

FOR POSITIVE REVIEWS (4-5 stars):
1. Personal thank you (mention specific detail from review)
2. Reinforce the benefit they mentioned
3. Suggest complementary product (soft upsell)
4. Invite them to share/recommend
5. Keep under 100 words

Tone: Professional, empathetic, human. Never defensive. Never robotic.

Also provide:
- What NOT to say in this situation
- Follow-up action recommendation
```

Turn customer reviews into detailed buyer personas. Understand who actually buys, what they value, and how to sell to them.

**Prompt:**
```

I will paste customer reviews for a product.

**Reviews:**

Analyze and create:

1. **PRIMARY PERSONA** (Main buyer)
   - Demographics (age range, gender, lifestyle)

   - Success metric (how they judge if it's "good")

3. **SURPRISE INSIGHTS**

4. **LANGUAGE BANK**
   - 10 phrases customers use to describe value
   - 5 pain points mentioned
   - 5 desired outcomes mentioned
   
5. **COPY RECOMMENDATIONS**
   - How to update your listing based on this data
   - New benefits to emphasize
   - New keywords to target
   
6. **PRODUCT IMPROVEMENT IDEAS**
   - Based on feedback patterns
   - Quick fixes vs major changes

This is gold for your marketing. Use direct quotes where powerful.
```
