## The Prompt

```
You are a content strategist who has helped creators build audiences from 0 to 100K+. You specialize in creating content calendars that balance consistency with quality.

Generate 30 UNIQUE CONTENT IDEAS based on my inputs:

=== MY CONTENT PROFILE ===

NICHE/TOPIC: [Your main subject area - be specific. "Fitness" is too broad, "Home workouts for busy parents" is better]

PLATFORM: [YouTube / TikTok / Instagram / Twitter / LinkedIn / Blog / Newsletter / Podcast]

TARGET AUDIENCE: 
- Who: [Age, profession, life stage]
- Their main struggle: [What problem do they have?]
- What they want: [What outcome are they seeking?]

MY UNIQUE ANGLE: [What makes your perspective different? Your background, contrarian view, specific experience]

CONTENT I'VE ALREADY MADE (to avoid repetition):
- [Past piece 1]
- [Past piece 2]
- [Past piece 3]

MY CONSTRAINTS:
- Time to create content: [1-2 hours/piece max / can go deep / varies]
- Comfort on camera: [very comfortable / audio only / prefer written]
- Can show my face: [yes / prefer not to / no]

=== GENERATE MY CONTENT CALENDAR ===

Create a table with 30 content ideas:

| # | Content Idea | Hook/Headline | Type | Effort | Potential |
|---|--------------|---------------|------|--------|-----------|

**Types to include (mix these):**
- Educational (teach a skill or concept)
- Story (personal experience or case study)
- Contrarian (challenge a common belief)
- Listicle (X ways to... / X mistakes...)
- Tutorial (step-by-step how to)
- Behind-the-scenes (your process)
- Trending + your take (timely content)
- Q&A / FAQ (answer common questions)
- Tool/resource share (helpful recommendations)
- Motivational (inspire or encourage)

**Effort levels:**
- 1 = Quick win (<1 hour to create)
- 2 = Standard (1-3 hours)
- 3 = Deep dive (3+ hours, high production)

**Potential ratings:**
- ⭐ = Solid foundational content
- ⭐⭐ = Good engagement likely
- ⭐⭐⭐ = High viral/share potential
```
