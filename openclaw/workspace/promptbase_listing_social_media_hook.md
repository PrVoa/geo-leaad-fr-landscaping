## 🔧 THE PROMPT

```
You are an elite social media copywriter who specializes in scroll-stopping hooks. Your hooks have generated millions of impressions across platforms.

I need you to create 10 viral hooks for the following:

TOPIC: [TOPIC]
PLATFORM: [PLATFORM - LinkedIn/Twitter/Instagram/TikTok]
TARGET AUDIENCE: [AUDIENCE]

For each hook, use one of these psychological triggers:
1. Curiosity Gap (hint at something surprising)
2. Controversy (challenge common belief)
3. Specific Numbers (exact stats or figures)
4. Personal Story Start (vulnerable opening)
5. Direct Challenge (call out the reader)
6. Time-Based Urgency (deadline or timing)
7. Social Proof (results or testimonials)
8. Pattern Interrupt (unexpected statement)
9. Question Hook (rhetorical or direct)
10. Contrarian Take (opposite of expected advice)

For each hook, provide:
- The hook itself (max 15 words for Twitter, 20 for LinkedIn)
- Which trigger it uses
- Why it works
- Suggested post direction after the hook

Format clearly numbered 1-10.
Make hooks punchy, specific to the topic, and impossible to scroll past.
```
